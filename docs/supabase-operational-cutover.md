# Operational Supabase cutover

## Scope and current reads

All nine D0 feeds stay in Supabase. The dedicated Backlog, Volume, KPI and KPI Targets modules keep their current sources. Accounts, authorization, configuration, feedback, and visit analytics keep their current storage.

The Lost compatibility paths now share the existing server-side `SUPABASE_LOST_SECRET_KEY` connection. `flmTeamGetSourceStatus` reads `lost_orders.ngay_chot`, `hub`, `spx_tracking`, and `created_at` for scoped availability, with the last field describing when a record entered the relation. The Lost part of `flmTeamGetHubOrders` maps `spx_tracking` to shipment ID, `ngay_chot` to the closing date, `ly_do` to reason, and `so_tien_den_bu` to the recorded amount. Unknown amounts remain null. Complete empty reads stay empty. A failed or incomplete read uses the established Sheets and BigQuery fallback without mixing rows from the two sources. When every applicable reader is unavailable, Lost source status reports an error and HUB detail fails with a retryable error. Failed results are not cached. The new reads constrain HUB names and dates in PostgREST and recheck HUB ownership through the existing permission directory before returning data. Scope coverage for additional source spellings needs read-only verification before release.

`flmTeamGetLostReport` and `flmTeamGetRiskSummary` already use `lost_orders` and `at_risk_daily_summary`. The existing Alert and Urgent Risk order feeds remain `alert_orders` and `urgent_orders`; their `date` and `deadline` fields define Risk dates, respectively. Keep the following legacy sources until their semantics are established.

- `flmTeamReadClaims_`, Claims source availability, and the urgent/alert branches of `flmTeamGetHubOrders` still need the legacy snapshot date, stage, explicit high-value and feedback breakdowns. The Risk summary has a source, station, and risk-day grain and exposes missing-feedback counts. A claim reason, current fleet status, or deadline does not establish the legacy snapshot dimensions.
- The Dashboard Lost aggregate uses legacy `(date, HUB, shipment)` attribution and reason groups. `lost_orders` has one row per tracking ID, and its daily and monthly pivots lack the legacy reason grain. Preserve the aggregate path until the owner reconciles shipments assigned to more than one HUB and confirms count, amount, and missing-amount parity.
- `% Loss Rate` still uses Sheets and its existing volume denominator. `Monthly Lost Volume by Hub` and the current `refreshLostBackend` outputs remain in place. There is no verified paid-settlement or debt source. Missing financial inputs remain unavailable.

## Proposed storage and transfer contracts

The create-only SQL draft is `supabase/drafts/operational_sources.sql`. It is outside the migrations directory and proposes a separate `spx_operational` schema whose application access and exposure require approval before loading data.

### People and attendance

- **Rider and Staff.** `PeopleApi.gs` reads `riders` and `staff` using `FLMP_snapshot_` and `FLMP_sourceRows_`. The draft keys snapshots by Vietnam capture date, source kind, and employee ID as text. Keep name, source station, mapped HUB, role, contract type, optional email, join/exit dates, and quality flags from the existing parser. Preserve the Staff exclusion for Rider positions and the conflict rules on duplicate IDs. Unmapped stations remain diagnosable to approved roles and outside HUB responses.
- **BPO.** `FLMP_bpoRows_` uses the original BPO ID, location, vendor, contractor type, last activity, idle days, and tag. Load one validated record per Vietnam capture date and BPO ID. Preserve the existing newest-activity choice and reject conflicting records with the same activity timestamp. Keep BPO separate from Rider and Staff employment data.
- **Raw attendance.** `d0WorkspaceSnapshot_` currently reads `BigQuery Attendance` and `d0AttendanceHours_` groups by Vietnam date, HUB and identity. Resolve the existing `person_key`, `biz_staff_id`, or `staff_id` to a consistent opaque key and retain the first clock-in instant and nullable late flag. Reject conflicting identities or clock-ins until the producer confirms its precedence rule. Resolve source station IDs through the approved existing HUB directory. Compare distinct attendance counts, hourly buckets, and null-clock-in counts with Sheets before switching. The existing daily and shift Supabase aggregates stay separate.

Use live stable exports and a fixed capture date or source revision for replay. Backfill historical People snapshots only where actual dated snapshots exist. The current People RPC may write its hidden `_People Resource Cache`; keep that cache and its existing write path during validation. Workforce IDs, names, email, and clock-ins stay in restricted storage, with the existing detail eligibility rules and a retention decision before production transfer.

### DTC, locations, and FC

- **DTC actuals.** `DTCAlerts.gs` reads backend `DTC D0` / `DTC` / `D0`; `Hubmaps.gs` additionally reads the frontend `DTC D0` copy. Preserve report date, HUB, computation time, backlog observation time, EOD backlog, 14-day average, DTC, status, band, coverage and window dates, and missing-date evidence. The draft retains multiple computations per HUB and report day; repeated imports of identical keys are idempotent. Conflicting rows with the same computation key or rows missing a trustworthy computation timestamp require a producer decision. Dedicated Backlog data remains an input under its existing source, and the DTC projection remains derived from DTC actuals plus the separately approved LM plan.
- **HubMap.** `getHubMap` reads the frontend HubMap aliases and combines coordinates with the independent DTC feed. Load one validated property record per existing canonical HUB code. Keep paired coordinates nullable and preserve address, ward, district, zone, type, and the source SQM label. Keep authorization and operational directory roles in the existing Permission sheets. Treat a location import as an idempotent update keyed by HUB code.
- **Forecast.** `dtcAlertReadFc_` reads configured FC workbooks with dated `#FM Forecast` and `#LM Forecast` columns. D0 uses the matching report day. DTC uses LM quantities and missing intervening days. `Code.gs` separately builds `Historical Forecast` / `_SYS_FORECAST_DAILY_FACT`. The draft publishes plan quantities by source, operating date, HUB, leg and immutable source revision. Capture publisher time in an import manifest if the workbook does not supply it. Confirm the revision order and retain the distinction between blank and zero. Actual inbound, pickup, line-haul, SOC, and calculated differences needed for historical forecast views stay with the existing pipeline pending a proven join and scope approval. Keep the dedicated FLM Volume joins to `flm_volume_snapshot`, `flm_backlog_daily`, and its independent BigQuery `fc_raw` unchanged.

## Approval and validation sequence

1. Confirm the producer for each feed, the source revision/capture policy, the retention and sensitive-field policy, and the canonical HUB crosswalk. The repository contains consumers, the legacy Forecast materialization, and an archived Rider repair workflow. It does not establish ownership or scheduling of the production workforce, attendance, DTC, location, or FC publishers.
2. Approve schema creation and the runtime access/exposure design separately. The draft contains only schema/table/index creation. It supplies no grants, RLS changes, backfill, triggers, credentials, or producer updates. Keep the new schema outside browser exposure until its read contract is approved.
3. Approve the source export, date range, idempotent transfer and reject review. Reconcile counts by date/HUB/source, duplicate keys, nulls, date and timezone conversions, role-filtered row sets, DTC capacity and coverage, and forecast blanks and zeroes. Document results before publishing any adapter change to production.
4. Approve the service configuration and cutover independently. Keep old publishers, caches, and triggers in place until each new reader has an agreed parity window and rollback criteria. Changing the People cache, the Forecast ETL, the Lost backend refresh, the Risk materialization refresh, or any production secret needs explicit approval.

The Lost and Risk server tests use synthetic fixtures. The draft is for review and has not been executed.
